| D26 | 13 Nov 2026 | Orders    | The buyer and delivery recipient may differ. Keep one order under the buyer; store recipient name, phone and address on that order. Confirmation by chat, WhatsApp, SMS link or staff, and delivery, do not merge contacts or replace buyer details. Count the purchase once under the buyer. No OTP is required.                                                                                                                                 |
| D27 | 13 Nov 2026 | Customers | Clarifies D26: for self-delivery, save the buyer's supplied own phone on their profile and the order snapshot, including during chat capture before an order exists. Saving does not verify ownership, merge contacts or grant marketing consent. If another contact already has that phone, keep it on the order and suggest a match for staff; do not overwrite profiles or block the order. Another recipient's phone stays only on the order. |
